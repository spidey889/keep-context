Browser extension · Preview

A simpler way
to connect your notes.

Sign in with Google. Approve ChatGPT. Ask about the notes you saved.

This is an unpublished preview for invited testing. Your trusted host supplies the extension and connection service. You do not need Python, a terminal, or a second password. Google credentials give broad account access; read how the connection works before connecting.

  1. Install Keep Context

    The setup page opens automatically. Enter your Google email, review the connection notice, then choose Agree and sign in with Google. Allow the one-time browser permission.

  2. Sign in with Google

    Complete Google's sign-in in the tab we opened. Keep Context finishes in the background and brings you back automatically. Even if Google stays loading after “I agree,” you do not need to press a Finish button.

  3. Connect ChatGPT

    Choose Connect ChatGPT and follow the setup page. On the connection page, check your Google account and choose Allow ChatGPT. When your notes are ready, select Keep Context in a new chat and ask “What do I still need to do?”

Installing this unpublished preview

Your host supplies the extension ZIP. Extract it, open Chrome or Brave's Extensions page, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select that folder. No pinning is needed. If setup is closed, choose Keep Context from the extensions menu.

A real ChatGPT listing link can open directly when your host provides one. Until publication, the setup page guides you through the one-time custom-server form and supplies Copy address and open ChatGPT. Use Keep Context as the name and leave OAuth/Dynamic Client Registration selected.

If something gets stuck

The setup tab closed

Open Keep Context from the extensions menu. Submitted progress and verified accounts are saved.

Your browser restarted

Reopen Keep Context. A verified account reconnects with its saved browser key. If Google sign-in had not finished, start that sign-in again.

Google sign-in expired or failed

Choose Start over, or sign in again when offered. Complete a fresh Google sign-in. You never need to find or copy a cookie yourself.

The ChatGPT request ended

Click Return to ChatGPT, then choose Connect again. Google Keep stays connected; you do not repeat Google sign-in. If your approval was saved, choose Finish ChatGPT connection instead.

You added a new note

Ask again after a minute. The next read refreshes the notes from Keep.

You want to disconnect

Use Manage connection → Disconnect Keep in the setup page to delete the hosted account and revoke ChatGPT's access. Removing the extension by itself does not delete that account. Google device/session access can be revoked separately in your Google account.

The earlier preview was manually verified through Google sign-in, ChatGPT approval and reading notes. This new guided setup still needs its fresh browser check. Consumer Google Keep access is unofficial and some accounts may be rejected. See what has been tested.
